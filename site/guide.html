Student guide

Use C.L.I.P. to join a class, answer checkpoints and revisit lesson material afterward.

Sign in and join

  1. Open the C.L.I.P. tab in Microsoft Teams with the student account your course uses. In My Learning, open your course and choose the live session. If you are already in the Teams meeting, open C.L.I.P. in the meeting side panel.
  2. Review the privacy notice and terms when prompted. Choose whether to enable optional camera and microphone indicators. You can answer checkpoints with both indicators off. See camera gaze and microphone activity for what the readings mean.
  3. If you see a waiting message, keep the panel open until the lecturer starts and links the class. Check that the course and session shown match your lesson.

Answer and ask for help

  1. When a checkpoint appears, select an option or write an answer, then submit before the timer ends. Check the submission status. When the question closes, read the feedback in the panel.
  2. When the Socratic tutor is available, ask it to explain a concept or give a hint. Open its numbered citations to read the lesson excerpt. Use discussion catch-up for material available in discussion notes.

Review after class

  1. From My Learning, open the completed session’s review. Select To revisit to find marked questions that need more practice. Use Show where this came from to open lesson excerpts.
  2. Use Progress to see topics across completed sessions. A missed checkpoint and an incorrect answer are shown separately.

Lecturer guide

Prepare checkpoint questions before class, then run the session and review the results.

Prepare the lesson

  1. Sign in with your lecturer account. Open Materials, choose the course and upload the lesson file. Wait for processing to finish before opening the generated questions.
  2. Review the drafts against the lesson source. Check the wording and correct answer or key points, then approve the questions you want available in class.
  3. Open Sessions and create a session for the course. Give it a recognizable title so students can find it.

Run the session in Teams

  1. Open the Teams meeting as the organiser and add C.L.I.P. from Apps if it is not already installed in that meeting. In the C.L.I.P. side panel, select the prepared session and choose Use in this meeting. Confirm the linked session before you start the class.
  2. Send an approved checkpoint from the live controls. Watch submitted answers and comprehension trends. Use this evidence to decide whether to revisit a concept before moving on.
  3. If you use breakout rooms, open them in Teams. Use C.L.I.P. to coordinate the room labels and student confirmations.

End and review

  1. Choose End class once the session is finished. Open the session results or report to review the recorded answers and comprehension trends.
  2. If discussion catch-up is available, review and publish your discussion notes so students can use them during revision.

Administrator guide

Use your assigned administrator account to set up courses and accounts and review operational and data requests.

Set up accounts and courses

  1. Open Accounts to find or create an account. Check its email and role before saving. Use Courses to link lecturers and students to the correct course.
  2. In Imports, choose the timetable or student roster. Review the preview and resolve flagged rows before confirming. After saving, verify the sessions or enrolments in the relevant course.

Review requests and records

  1. Open Special consideration to review pending requests. Check the requested accommodation and review any supporting evidence before recording a decision.
  2. In Data governance, use the retention preview to see which records are due for removal and use the audit log to review recorded actions. The retention purge runs automatically.
  3. For a deletion request, verify the target and request status. A different administrator must approve the request before it can be executed. Check the outcome in Data governance.

Check service health

  1. Open System status to check whether the database and model service are available. If a service is unavailable, record the time and on-screen status for the project operator.

Troubleshooting

The meeting panel is unavailable

Confirm you are in the intended Teams account and meeting. Ask the organiser to add C.L.I.P. to that meeting. If the app still fails to load, send the course name, time and on-screen error to your lecturer or project support.

A checkpoint or source is missing

Open the side panel and check the connection and session title. If a checkpoint has closed, review your results after class. If a source is unavailable, use the lesson material provided by the lecturer.

The tutor or optional indicators are missing

The tutor and indicators depend on the deployment and class settings. You can still complete the class workflow and answer checkpoints. Camera and microphone indicators use separate consent.

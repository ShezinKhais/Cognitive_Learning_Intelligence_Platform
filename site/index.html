Before, during, and after class

Before class

Prepare the material

The lecturer uploads lesson files. C.L.I.P. drafts questions from the material and records the source. A lecturer reviews, edits, and approves the questions before using them.

During class

Run live checkpoints

Students answer multiple-choice or written questions in the Teams side panel. The lecturer follows submissions and comprehension trends, so they can decide when to explain a concept again.

After class

Review and revise

Students see their own answers, feedback, and source excerpts in Session Review. The Socratic tutor offers guided hints and questions. The lecturer can review the class results and export a report.

Optional student indicators

Camera gaze and microphone activity

When enabled and permitted, the browser uses MediaPipe Face Landmarker to estimate whether a face is visible and whether its direction is consistent with looking toward the screen. A short calibration can help when the camera is positioned off-centre.

Voice activity detection estimates whether someone is speaking. The browser processes both indicators locally. It sends the share of time facing the screen, face presence, a speech flag, and the time window. C.L.I.P. combines these readings into minute summaries.

Camera position, low light, glasses, or a face out of view can affect the estimate. The indicators describe limited participation signals. Checkpoint answers provide the evidence for comprehension.

Tools for each role

Students
Join classes, answer checkpoints, read feedback, and ask for hints grounded in lesson sources. After class, use To revisit and Progress over time to find topics that need practice.
Lecturers
Manage materials, approve questions, start and link lessons in Teams, and see live class trends. Manage the rooms in Teams and use C.L.I.P. to coordinate room labels and confirmations.
Administrators
Manage accounts and courses, preview timetable and roster imports, and review special consideration and data requests. System status and the audit log help administrators investigate issues.

Data and consent

What C.L.I.P. processes

Student camera and microphone

Each optional indicator requires separate consent. The browser keeps raw camera frames and microphone audio from these indicators on the device. Students can switch them off and continue answering questions. Indicators do not affect marks.

Discussion notes and lecturer transcription

Catch-up uses discussion notes made available to students. If the deployment enables live lecturer transcription, the lecturer can start it after confirming notice and consent. Short microphone chunks go to the C.L.I.P. server for speech recognition. Stored transcript text is distinct from student voice activity.

Session records

Answers, feedback, and summaries are subject to the configured retention period. By default, eligible session data is purged after 90 days. Students can use My data to download their data or request deletion.

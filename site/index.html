How a lesson works

01

Prepare

The lecturer uploads materials and reviews generated questions before making them available for class.

02

Check understanding

Students answer checkpoints in the Teams meeting side panel. The lecturer can see response trends during class.

03

Review and study

Students revisit their answers, open the related lesson excerpts and ask the Socratic tutor for guided help.

Understanding and engagement are separate.

Checkpoint answers help students see what they understood. Optional camera and microphone indicators give limited signals about participation. They require separate consent, are processed on the student’s device, and send only derived signals to C.L.I.P.

Lecturer microphone transcription is a separate, optional feature. When enabled, short audio chunks go to the server for processing. Read the in-app Privacy notice for how text is shared and retained.
